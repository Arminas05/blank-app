60-day guarantee

You have 60 days from the day the brush is delivered — not the day you ordered — to decide it isn't for you. Email hello@cindergrill.com with your order number and one line about what went wrong. We refund the full amount you paid, including any shipping charge and the import duty that sits inside the price.

Condition

Used is fine. Greasy, scratched, box long gone: it makes no difference to the refund. A guarantee that requires unused condition on a cleaning tool is not a guarantee.

Returning the product

For most refunds we do not ask for the product back. Return carriage to our manufacturing partner in Guangdong costs more than the brush is worth, so we refund you and you keep it or pass it on. If you would rather return it, we will send a US address and cover the postage where the fault is ours.

Cancellations

Orders are sent to the factory in batches twice a day. Email within 12 hours of ordering and we cancel and refund in full before anything is packed. After that the order falls under the 60-day guarantee instead.

Warranty

Separately from the guarantee, the motor and the battery cell carry a 12-month warranty from delivery. If the motor stops or the cell will not hold a charge in that window we ship a replacement and nothing needs to come back. Wire wear is normal use and is not covered, nor is damage caused by using the tool on a hot grill.

Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method and typically appear within 3 to 5 business days of us issuing them. No restocking fee is charged and nothing is deducted.
