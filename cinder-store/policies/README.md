# Store policies

Paste each file into **Settings → Policies**. Use Shopify's built-in policy
pages rather than a custom template — checkout links to them automatically, and
a custom page will not be linked from the checkout footer.

| File                     | Shopify field            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| `refund-policy.html`     | Refund policy            |
| `shipping-policy.html`   | Shipping policy          |
| `privacy-policy.html`    | Privacy policy           |
| `terms-of-service.html`  | Terms of service         |

## Before you publish these

`privacy-policy.html` and `terms-of-service.html` are **placeholders**. They
describe what this store actually does, which is more than Shopify's generator
gives you, but they are not legal advice and they have not been reviewed by a
lawyer. Handoff §7 item 3 lists this as a launch blocker and it still is.

The refund and shipping policies are safer: they are a restatement of the
`pages/returns.html` and `pages/shipping.html` copy, which describes decisions
you have already made. They still need to match what you will genuinely honour
before you take money.
