What we collect

Your name, email address, shipping address and order history, so we can send you a brush and answer your email about it. If you contact us, we keep that correspondence. Our store runs on Shopify, which also collects standard traffic and device data.

Payment details

Card details are handled by Shopify's payment processor and never reach our systems. We can see the last four digits and the card brand on an order, nothing more.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, rent your email address, or add you to a marketing list you did not ask to join.

Sharing

We share your name and shipping address with our manufacturing and fulfilment partner and the carriers who move the parcel, because otherwise it cannot be delivered. Customs authorities receive the declaration data the route requires.

Retention and deletion

If you ask to be forgotten we delete what we can and tell you what tax and accounting law requires us to keep, and for how long.
