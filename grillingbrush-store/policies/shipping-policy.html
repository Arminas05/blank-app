Where we ship from

Orders are built, packed and dispatched by our manufacturing partner in Guangdong, China, and flown direct to the destination country.

Processing

One to two business days to pick, pack and hand the parcel to the carrier. Orders placed on Friday afternoon leave on Monday. Delivery estimates begin at the first carrier scan, not at checkout.

Import duty and taxes

We ship delivered-duty-paid. The United States ended the $800 de minimis exemption on 29 August 2025, so every parcel is dutiable. Duty and import handling are settled by us before the parcel moves and are included in the price shown at checkout. You will not be billed by the courier on delivery.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed at the first carrier scan, usually three to five days after ordering. Tracking then goes quiet for several days during consolidation and air freight before updating again at customs clearance. This is normal on this route.

Address restrictions

The tool contains an installed lithium-ion cell and ships as UN3481. We cannot deliver to PO boxes or to APO/FPO addresses. Please supply a street address.

Seasonal closure

The factory and the freight network close for approximately two weeks around the lunar new year, usually late January into February. Orders placed in that window ship when it reopens.

Lost or damaged parcels

If 30 business days pass with no delivery scan, contact hello@grillingbrush.com with your order number and we will replace or refund the order at your choice. If the parcel arrives damaged, a photograph is enough; nothing needs to be returned.
